The product

Travel Day Organizer turns a complex multi-island trip into a calm, hour-by-hour plan. Four surfaces — an overview, a month calendar, a day timeline, and a packing/documents tracker — share one consistent visual language: warm cards, a friendly event-icon system, and motion that's functional, not decorative.

Motion

Every animation is a pure function of progress, so each loop is seamless. The event icons each carry a motion that matches what they are; selection feels tactile; the "now" marker keeps the timeline alive.
